Numaya illustration catalog

Every place a Numaya illustration belongs, shown on the real screens with the area outlined. Make each PNG with the exact file name, put it in src/assets/illustrations/, and it replaces the slot's fallback on the next build. Details: README · asset guidelines · manifest.

  • 14PNG assets
  • 18placements
  • 1P0 essential
  • 8P1 high value
  • 5P2 optional

Not illustrations (not listed here): Profile photos (dynamic content: the Match portrait, cards, avatars); Navigation and utility icons (Material Symbols Rounded); Profile readiness meter and progress bars (data components); Activity and Home photo placeholders (photography, not illustration).

numaya-match-hands-heart.png

P0 · required ID match-hands-heart Code IllustrationSlot id="match-hands-heart"

PurposeThe match moment itself: two people meeting halfway and making something together.
Art directionTwo hands entering from the left and right edges, meeting in the centre to form a heart with fingers and thumbs. Friendly, slightly stylised (not photoreal), one hand wearing a couple of rings, the other a bracelet or watch. The heart shape should read at 120px tall. Arms may bleed off the left and right edges of the canvas.
PaletteWhite or transparent ground; near-black (#141316) outlines; brand purple #7262C7 as the one strong accent; lavender #F3F0FD / #E9E4FB for soft fills; Meetup periwinkle, yellow and rose only as small marks. Skin tones: a warm and a cool tone so the hands read as two different people.
Aspect ratio5:2 (wide)
Export size@1x 320 × 128 · @2x 640 × 256 · @3x 960 × 384 (deliver this)
Safe marginsHeart centred with 12% clear space above and below; arms may touch the left and right edges.
BackgroundTransparent PNG (sits on white).
ShadowsNone in the artwork; the UI adds none either.
TextNo embedded text.
AnimationPlanned: hands slide in from the edges and meet to form the heart (separate layers for each hand and accessory in the source file).
Status todayfallback: joining purple heart (MatchHeart)
