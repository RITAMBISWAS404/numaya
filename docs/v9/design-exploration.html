Ten decisions for V9

Each section names the problem, shows two or three directions drawn with Numaya's own tokens (brand #7262C7, Plus Jakarta Sans headings over Figtree text, the V7 radius scale), and records the one that ships. The app contains only the recommended option; the others stay here for review.

Photos are drawn as colour fields on purpose: the member photos used in the app are private and are not published in this file.

1. Typography

Foundations · D-01

V8 replaced the V7 faces with Bree Serif titles over Google Sans. On review the serif read as a different product, and it broke mid-word at large text in the Compliment composer. The brief asks to return to the type that worked before.

Expressive, but a second voice the Figma file never had; one weight only.

B · V7 restoredRecommended

Plus Jakarta Sans for headings, names and numbers; Figtree for reading text and controls, at the Ente sizes the Figma file defines.

Why B: it is the established identity, it matches the Figma text styles (Display-1 32/40, Display-2 24/32, H2 18/24, Body 15/20), and both faces are variable, so hierarchy can come from weight. Kept from V8: times inside message bubbles and the one-line chat header name.

5. Text over photos

Discover · Match · Profile · D-03

A fixed 24% gradient left white names faint on bright photos (snow, beaches, white walls), while a stronger fixed one darkens every dark photo for nothing.

Photo:
A · Fixed 24%V8
Maya, 29Product designer

B · AdaptiveRecommended
Maya, 29Product designer

C · Glass labelAlternative
Maya, 29

Always readable, but a dark box on every photo; heavier than the photo-first look.

Why B: the app measures the lower 40% of each photo once (decoded at 12×16, cached) and sets the gradient's bottom edge between 24% (dark photos) and 52% (white photos). The gradient covers only the band under the text and eases to clear, so the rest of the photo is untouched. The name keeps its two soft text shadows.

7. Icons

Iconography · D-07

Some cards put their icon on a tinted square, and actionable banners had a grey disc behind their arrow: decoration that adds visual weight without meaning.

A · BackplatesV8
♥Compliment credits
B · Glyph on the surfaceRecommended
♥ Compliment credits

Small icons use the filled glyph for legibility; row icons stay outline.

Why B: Airbnb's profile rows and Apple Health's highlights both draw the glyph straight on the surface. Backplates stay only where they do work: the send button, the photo action buttons (contrast over images), selection marks and the swipe stamps.

10. Tab switching

Motion · D-09

Changing tabs was a hard cut. Tabs must keep their scroll and state, so the motion cannot rebuild them.

A · CutV8
B · 200ms fade inRecommended
C · SlideRejected
Why B: the new tab fades in over 200ms ease-out. Nothing moves, so it is comfortable for motion-sensitive people; with reduced motion it is instant. Slide implies an order between tabs that does not exist. Pushes keep their 280ms emphasized curve with a quicker 200ms return, and presses still respond on touch-down.
