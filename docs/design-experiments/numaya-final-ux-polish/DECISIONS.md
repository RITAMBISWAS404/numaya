# Decisions: final UX polish

These continue the premium refinement log
([PR-01 to PR-16](../monochrome-meetup-premium/DECISIONS.md)). Nothing here reverts an approved
choice. The palette, `#7262C7`, Plus Jakarta Sans over Figtree, the radius scale, the tab pill and
the filled controls all stay.

| # | Decision | Why | Rejected |
|---|---|---|---|
| FP-01 | **Semantic accents only in icon tiles, status marks and illustrations.** The `AccentTone` set is brand (purple), info (blue `#2C5C99`), positive (green `#2B774B`), caution (amber `#8A5A00`), social (coral `#A8402F`), danger (red) and neutral. Every foreground is ≥ 4.5:1 on its own tint and on white. | The app read as lifeless grey. Colour now helps scanning without turning into a dashboard. Purple remains the only action colour. | Coloured buttons or cards; a colour per row; gradients |
| FP-02 | **Grouped lists get quiet hairlines again** (`divider` `#ECECF0`). They start where the row's text starts, never through the tile, and stop at the card padding. Groups can opt out. | Supersedes PR-08 for grouped lists. Tiled rows without separators blurred together on long screens (Settings, Account Health). The brief asks for subtle separators aligned to the text. | Full-width lines; heavier `line` colour |
| FP-03 | **`ListRow.leading` renders a 40pt rounded-square `IconTile`** (12 corner, 20pt icon, neutral by default); `inlineIcon` keeps a bare icon for plain metadata. Profile tiles and the Premium card use the 48pt size. | One component gives one rhythm: every row's text starts on the same line. Rounded squares match the 16pt header buttons better than circles. | Circles; tiles on every row of every list (avatars and plain value rows stay as they are) |
| FP-04 | **Account Health rows carry status in the tile:** a green check when done, an amber clock when something is missing, blue while photo verification is pending. The value text says the same thing in words. | Account Health "below the hero is still a parts list" (last QA). Status is now scannable, still without invented metrics (PR-11), and colour is never the only signal. | A score; progress rings per row |
| FP-05 | **Match shows only the matched person.** It is one square photo with the hero-card corner (32), up to 320pt wide and never taller than 40% of the screen, with a purple heart mark on its lower edge. Without a photo, their initial stands in. The member's own avatar and the scalloped two-avatar composition are gone. | Explicit brief. Feeld and Bumble give the other person the moment; the self-avatar added nothing. | A frame or extra shapes around the photo; animating the mark |
| FP-06 | **Nested corners follow `inner = max(0, outer − inset)`** (`AppRadii.nested`). The tab tint becomes a circle (32 − 6 = 26 on a 52pt item), as NAVIGATION_SPEC already said. The RSVP bar's corner becomes the button's 28 + 12 = 40. The Discover photo stays 32 − 8 = 24. | The tab indicator had drifted to a 16pt square inside a pill, and the RSVP bar's 24 corner sat outside its 28 pill button. | One radius everywhere |
| FP-07 | **The rule applies only when the curves are visibly related** (inset ≤ outer radius). Content far inside a card (an illustration 24–32pt in) keeps its own role radius. | Computing 24 − 24 = 0 would square off art that is not concentric with anything. | Applying the formula blindly |
| FP-08 | **Swipe feedback names the decision on the photo:** a white disc with a check (Like, purple) or a cross (Pass, ink) plus the word, fading in from a quarter of the threshold. | Bumble's check is the convention people already know. Icon plus word plus direction means three channels. | Coloured photo tint (hides the photo); text badge only |
| FP-09 | **A swipe never decides by accident.** A system pointer cancel (call, notification shade) springs the card back, even past the line. So does a firm fling back toward the centre. A second action on a person already being decided is ignored (`_deciding`, plus a check against `seen`). | Found while writing the tests: Flutter ends a cancelled drag with zero velocity, so a card past the line was liked. A double tap could also act twice. | Only blocking during the exit animation (the previous guard) |
| FP-10 | **Undo stays pass-only, one step.** | This is Bumble's Backtrack behaviour and the existing rule. A like has already been sent. | Undoing likes |
| FP-11 | **Every Settings row gets a tile.** Neutral by default; blue for visibility and messages, coral for likes, purple for Travel, amber for the prototype error switch. | Half the screen had icons and half did not. | Icons only where they existed already |
| FP-12 | **Copy shortened where tiles made it wrap:** Snooze becomes "Pause without losing matches", Profile & visibility becomes "Completeness and notices", Your area becomes "Needed for distances to people and events.", and the Likes card caption becomes "Liked you · 4 km away" (a non-breaking space keeps "4 km" together). | Each was one line longer than needed at 393pt; the Likes caption left "km" alone on a line. Dynamic content (names, titles, messages) is never truncated. | Ellipsis on authored copy |
| FP-13 | **The host avatar on an event is 40pt**, the tile width. | Every row's text then starts on the same line, and the divider inset matches. | 32pt avatar beside 40pt tiles |
| FP-14 | **Goldens regenerated on Windows after review.** All 187 changed: the previous set was rendered on Linux, and font rasterising differs. The before/after evidence renders both sides on the same machine. | So that the comparison is honest | Comparing against the Linux goldens |
