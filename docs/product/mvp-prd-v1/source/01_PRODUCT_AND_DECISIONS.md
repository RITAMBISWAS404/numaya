# Product requirements
## Objective and audience
India-first adult dating: discover, express interest, reciprocate, talk. Primary recruitment positioning: adults 22–35; eligibility remains 18+. Nationwide availability with concentrated recruitment campaigns. Two-person operating team. Pitch goal: polished demonstrable experience and, if release gates pass, invite-only real-user pilot.
Differentiation hypothesis: understandable profile readiness and activity insights, limited contextual introductions, lightweight rewarded participation. Validate usefulness; do not claim market uniqueness or guaranteed matches.
## Owner-approved scope
Four tabs: Discover, Likes, Chats, Profile. Dashboard inside Profile. Phone OTP only. Minimum two, maximum six photos; required short bio and one prompt, up to three prompts. Relationship/casual/figuring-it-out intentions. Age/distance/gender preferences free; intention and lifestyle filtering Premium. Intention remains visible to everyone.
Free five positive likes/day, Premium fifteen. Passes free. Ordinary mutual matches allow either party to initiate, expire after24h without first message. First message stops initial timer. Seven days without any conversation message triggers check-in; fourteen days expires chat. Preserve history, disable sends. Either party may request reconnection once; acceptance required and free. Block/report/unmatch always free.
One free24h match extension/week, Premium one/day; one extension total per match. No extensions to rejected/blocked connections.
Compliments:150 characters, on a photo or prompt answer only; one pending intro per pair, visible in Chats requests. Sender cannot send again until recipient replies. Reply creates reciprocal conversation atomically. No pending request expiry in MVP. Dismiss/block/report available. Credit spent only on accepted send.
Streak day: three distinct profile decisions OR one sent message in reciprocal conversation. When neither opportunity exists, explicit profile-review completion qualifies. No15-minute dwell requirement. Seven consecutive qualifying days awards one credit. Paid recovery can repair one missed day within48h, once per seven-day cycle. No duplicate reward after recovery.
Phone-only verification badge is not identity verification. No online/last-active/read receipts. Chat text and emoji only. Events, SuperSwipe, calls, attachments, boosts, Incognito and photo analytics deferred.
## Delegated defaults
Welcome three credits once after eligible profile completion. Earned/welcome bank cap5; award at cap is skipped with clear explanation, no purchase required. Premium weekly allowance3 resets weekly, no rollover. Purchased credits separate, persist until used while account exists. Spend weekly first, earned next, purchased last. Credits already earned survive streak reset. Pending intro dismissed does not refund; technical failure does.
Age gate before collecting dating profile. Device approximate location while in use; manual-city fallback; no background trail. City/coarse distance display, never exact coordinates. All pairings have equal initiation rights. Pilot phone verification plus photo moderation and manual suspicious-account review.
## Commercial and member review personas
Business analyst (synthetic review role, not interviewed research): seeks sustainable net revenue, low OTP/storage/support costs, adequate local supply, measurable conversion. Member advocate (synthetic): seeks relevant people, understandable limits, autonomy, safety, no shame or reply coercion.
Debate resolutions: finite likes protect supply but cannot prevent exhaustion; never sell access when inventory is empty. Analytics sell depth, not essential restriction explanations. Timer creates urgency but history and free consensual reconnection reduce loss. Paid streak repair is optional; earned credits remain intact. Premium buys allowances and information, not a ranking guarantee. Limited supply requires recruitment, not merely restrictive quotas.
Success: reciprocal conversation rate, first-reply rate, seven-day retention, report resolution time, refund rate, paid conversion and contribution margin. No invented numeric success claims; establish pilot baseline and pre-register experiment targets.
