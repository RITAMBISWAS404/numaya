# Health and data specification
Delegated formula v1, versioned and explained: required identity/preferences/intention30; two approved photos20; required bio15; first prompt15; optional third photo10; optional second prompt10. No mandatory sensitive optional disclosure. Score never changes ranking and cannot be purchased. Required-complete profile begins80. Label Profile Health with subtitle Profile setup; no medical or attractiveness implications.
Discovery status separate: visible/paused/incomplete/under review/restricted/unknown. Missing service response must show unknown, not restricted. Always disclose actionable account restrictions for free without exposing reporter/security details.
Free: score, full formula, setup actions, visibility status and last7day aggregate likes/matches/conversations. Premium:28/90day trends and meaningful exposure-to-like rates when denominator is sufficient. Candidate threshold20 distinct qualified exposures; label low sample below threshold. Threshold is product heuristic, not statistical certainty. No causal claim that a specific photo, nonreply or unmatch caused low reach.
Qualified impression: authenticated eligible other user sees at least50% of profile card for1second; deduplicate viewer/profile/day. This event can be imperfect; label approximate exposure. Never reveal identities via analytics. Exclude internal demo/test/bots where identified, own views, blocked pairs. No cross-app tracking, contacts scraping, message sentiment scoring or continuous location tracking.
Data: private auth phone/DOB; public display profile and age; private coordinates; per-user ledger; pair likes/matches/messages; event aggregate tables; moderation cases restricted to authorized staff. Log event names/timestamps and pseudonymous IDs, never OTP or message bodies in analytics. Reporting may include explicitly selected message evidence.
Proposed retention: raw analytics30days, aggregates90days, expired chat history90days after expiry with advance notice and report-preservation exception. Account deletion hides immediately; target primary-data deletion within30days, backups age out within90days, legally required records separately access-controlled. These are proposed policies requiring vendor/legal validation before live release, not assertions of statutory deadlines.
India DPDP obligations and commencement phases require launch-date legal review. Document consent/purpose, processors, cross-border handling, withdrawal/export/deletion, incident response and grievance owner. Location permission is not blanket consent for all analytics. Nonessential analytics opt-out must not remove core service.
Business metrics: net revenue less store/payment fees, taxes borne, refunds, OTP, hosting/storage and variable support cost; calculate contribution margin by cohort. Do not call gross revenue profit. Track meaningful reciprocal conversations, not just time spent. Safety metrics and complaint/unsubscribe rates are guardrails.
