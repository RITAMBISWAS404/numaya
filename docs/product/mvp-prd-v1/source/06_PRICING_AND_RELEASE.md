# Commercial proposal and release gates
Delegated provisional India price hypotheses: Premium weekly INR99, monthly INR299, yearly INR1999; three Compliments INR79; one eligible recovery INR19. These are NOT researched competitor price matches or approved store products. Weekly annualizes higher: disclose full billing term and total, no fake savings/countdown. Validate willingness to pay, live storefront alternatives, store-supported price points, taxes/fees and margins before activating billing. Configurable server catalog, never hard-code economic policy in widgets.
One tier:15likes/day,3weeklyCompliments, incoming profiles, advanced filters, daily extension, deeper insights. Free core chat/report/reconnect remains usable. No ads or extra-like packs. Paid recovery added as owner-approved exception to otherwise minimal consumables.
Pilot: invite-only if readiness passes; open India signup can be waitlisted until operations/capacity ready. Demo APK may use clearly labelled sample data. Do not collect money against mock entitlements or market synthetic analytics as real.
Release gates: real OTP delivery/abuse controls; two-account discovery/match/chat; authorization tests; moderation queue and responsible human; age gate and underage escalation; account deletion and privacy notice; crash/error monitoring; backups/restore; store billing verification if enabled; signing ownership and secure keys; applicable store dating/child-safety policy review. Disable incomplete paid features. A local debug APK is for review, not public-production certification.
Growth: review quotas and prices against local eligible inventory, reciprocal conversations, conversion, costs and complaints. No automatic tightening simply because scale increases. Honor paid-through benefits, announce changes and avoid confiscating earned/purchased balances.
