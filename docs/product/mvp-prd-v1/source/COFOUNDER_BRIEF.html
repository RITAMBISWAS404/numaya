Numaya • Product decision brief • 7 November 2026 • Proposed implementation specification

Numaya: the MVP

India-first18+ dating with Discover, Likes, Chats and Profile. Phone OTP. Two to six photos, a required bio and at least one prompt. Keep the approved15px purple/Tabler Ente-inspired design. Events and advanced media wait.

How connection works

Free5 likes/day; Premium15. Either person starts a mutual match within24h. One extension per match. Compliments are one150-character photo/prompt intro; recipient reply opens chat. Quiet conversations get a reminder after7days and expire after14; history remains and free consensual reconnection is available.

Health and rewards

Profile Health explains setup readiness, not attractiveness or a hidden punishment. Actual visibility and outcomes shown separately. Free gets essential explanations and weekly summary; Premium gets deeper trends. Seven qualifying days earns one Compliment. Three welcome credits; earned bank cap5. Premium3 weekly credits; purchases separate. Optional paid recovery repairs one missed day within48h.

Business and fairness

One Premium tier; weekly/monthly/yearly. Proposed prices99/299/1999 INR need validation. Three-credit pack79 and recovery19 are hypotheses. Core chat and safety remain free. Track contribution margin after costs, meaningful conversations, retention and complaints. No guarantee of matches or profitability.

Delivery and responsibility

Build from the latest approved local checkpoint on a separate branch. Deliver an APK, screenshots, test evidence and pushed handoff. Real onboarding is an invite-only pilot only after authentication, authorization, reporting/moderation, deletion and operational checks. Co-founder owns backend/operations; design founder owns UI. Mock data must be labelled. Pricing and public launch require validation.
