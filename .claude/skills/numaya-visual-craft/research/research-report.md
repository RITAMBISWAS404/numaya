# Tim Gabe research and Numaya visual-craft skill

**Research date:** 25 October 2026, Asia/Kolkata.
**Requested publication window:** 25 April–25 October 2026 inclusive.
**Recent priority window:** 25 July–25 October 2026.
**Deliverable status:** Standalone Claude Code skill and controlled pilot plan prepared. No Numaya repository was modified. The skill has not been tested on Numaya's real screens.

## Executive finding

The strongest in-window material supports a methodology built around coherent art direction, consistent visual foundations, meaningful treatment of core moments, and complete product states. It does **not** support claiming a comprehensive Tim Gabe recipe for exact typography, spacing, shadows, or photographic composition.

The package therefore separates verified creator explanations from researcher synthesis. It translates the former into a practical inspect–diagnose–direct–implement–render–compare loop and adds explicit Numaya-specific craft rules with honest provenance.

## 1. Collection identification

The investigation began at the actual [Tim Gabe Videos listing](https://www.youtube.com/@TimGabe/videos), sorted Latest. The search reader returned only a footer, so a browser was used to inspect the real listing. The visible channel showed 165 videos and 168K subscribers at inspection; these are a snapshot, not a completeness guarantee.

The listing supplied titles, watch IDs, and relative age. Individual watch pages were then opened and expanded to obtain exact displayed publication dates, descriptions, chapter controls, and transcript availability. Relative “6 months” labels were not used to establish eligibility.

Sixteen identified Videos-tab uploads fell in the requested window, from May 6 through October 22. The next older upload was April 17, outside the April 25 boundary. Four further boundary candidates were inspected through March 31. No claim is made about deleted/private/unlisted videos, Shorts, Live uploads, or subsequent uploads later on October 25.

The [source index](../references/video-source-index.md) contains every identified in-window title, date, verified watch URL, inclusion/exclusion decision, and the five boundary candidates.

## 2. Selection criteria

Selection favored (1) demonstrated visual-craft or redesign reasoning, (2) actionable transfer to Numaya, (3) accessible actual captions or visual evidence, and then (4) recency. Subscriber/view counts did not determine rank.

Eight sources were retained, five from the recent priority window:

1. **The Only Formula Behind Truly Great Apps** — Sep 1: strongest direct art-direction and AI-workflow material.
2. **How Top 1% Apps Earn Your Second Visit** — Oct 7: strongest visual-identity and emotional-design case.
3. **The Psychology of Premium Apps** — Oct 15: whole-product quality and state completeness.
4. **I Redesigned a Neobank App (Here's Why People Started Funding It)** — Oct 22: brand continuity, trustworthy status, and restraint; fintech-specific material used selectively.
5. **How Top 1% Indie Apps Beats Giants** — Jun 11: core-surface personality and accumulated craft.
6. **How To Solve The App Onboarding Paradox** — Jun 3: a narrow lesson about meaningful work and personalization.
7. **The Weird Design Playbook of 6 App Outliers** — Jun 25: audience-specific representation and emotional context.
8. **The Secret Behind Apps Big Players Can't Copy** — Oct 11: contextual category-assumption lens, not a visual tutorial.

This is not eight equally strong UI tutorials. The last three support specific product-context decisions. Exact links, priorities, transcript routes, and timestamps are in the source index.

Paywalls, leaderboards, and generic retention content were deprioritized. **Why Irresistible Apps Are Designed This Way (It's Genius)** was read in captions and excluded because its clock/loss mechanics, including the Hinge example, address return behavior more than static visual craft. Dating relevance alone was insufficient.

**Our World Class App Design Formula**, despite an appealing title and “6 months” listing label, was dated April 2 and excluded. No lesson from it was included in the methodology.

## 3. Material actually accessed

- Expanded YouTube descriptions and dates for all 16 in-window candidates and five boundary candidates.
- English auto-generated captions exported and read for the eight selected sources.
- Captions additionally read for the excluded clock-mechanics video.
- Captions exported but not analyzed for the July identity video and May gamification video; no knowledge claim rests on those transcripts.
- Captions exported for the April 2 boundary candidate during eligibility checking, but not used in synthesis.
- Selected actual playback frames inspected in TG01, TG02, and TG04, detailed below.
- Primary Claude Code documentation for project skills and W3C documentation for relevant web accessibility checks.

Most descriptions were brief agency-call promotions; they were poor substitutes for captions. Direct search-service watch-page requests were throttled/disabled, while browser access succeeded. An initially premature neobank transcript export failed, then succeeded after loading. No video files were downloaded.

Auto-captions are not human-reviewed transcripts. They contain apparent name/transcription errors. Product names not independently verified were qualified. Numerical growth and psychological claims are treated as creator-reported or unverified, and no outcome statistic becomes an implementation requirement.

## 5. Best-supported recurring findings

### Direction is more useful than a pile of references

TG01 directly compares guided and overloaded AI design approaches; TG02 turns a desired feeling into a concrete visual foundation. **High confidence that these are the creator's positions.** The generalization that one compatible direction is a useful coding-agent constraint is our synthesis, with moderate confidence until tested.

### Quality should hold across the product

TG01's consistency axis, TG02's shared foundation, TG03's connected-state argument, and TG04's recurring product object all support coherence across screens. **High source confidence.** This motivates a shared-component audit and a five-screen comparison, not isolated screenshot beauty.

### Personality belongs in the core surface and meaningful moment

TG02's recap treatment and TG05's core-surface personality argument support deliberate emotional presentation. **High confidence in the described rationale; moderate confidence in visual conclusions** because only limited frames were inspected. Applying this to a mutual-match composition is an inference, not Tim Gabe discussing Numaya.

### Completeness contributes to perceived quality

TG03 and TG04 repeatedly discuss real status, clear explanations, control, and recovery. **High source confidence.** Their business-effect claims are not causal evidence here. The skill checks these states while keeping the first experiment centered on static visual craft.

### Audience and purpose should constrain conventions

TG06–TG08 support asking why a step or representation exists before copying category norms. **Moderate relevance to this task.** These sources do not authorize changing Numaya's core model or adding engagement mechanisms.

## 6. What was synthesized, not verified as Tim Gabe doctrine

The detailed methods for spacing groups, optical alignment, portrait crops, exact accent roles, component geometry, semantic tokens, reference selection limits, screenshot setup, and the 13-dimension rubric are researcher-created operational adaptations.

Fraunces, Inter, premium white/neutral backgrounds, purple/lavender accents, connection-focused purpose, and consistent functional icons come from the user's brief. Exact repository token values, assets, layout, and screen routes remain unknown.

The package makes no claim that premium means more blur, gradients, glass, shadows, large radii, artwork, or animation. Its static-first order is a deliberate experimental control. Some source examples use motion, but the first pilot intentionally does not implement it.

Accessibility criteria come from the linked W3C primary documentation, not the creator. The suggested 44×44 touch target is a local preference, distinguished from WCAG AA's 24×24 CSS-pixel minimum with exceptions.

## 7. Why this skill structure

- **SKILL.md:** A compact operational entry point with repository grounding, identity constraints, diagnosis, reference selection, implementation, iteration, and finish criteria.
- **visual-principles.md:** Concrete problem indicators, improvements, and counterexamples across the requested visual categories, with provenance labels.
- **video-source-index.md:** Ranked shortlist, dates, URLs, timestamps, caption reliability, catalogue decisions, and access limits.
- **visual-case-studies.md:** Concise examples separating observed frames from narrated changes and Numaya interpretations.
- **visual-review-checklist.md:** Repeatable observations, anchored scoring, accessibility/behavior gates, and keep/refine/revert rules.
- **five-screen-experiment-plan.md:** Route mapping, fixed fixtures/viewports, screen-specific hypotheses, regression checks, and pending results.
- **research-report.md:** Research audit and confidence limits kept out of the operational workflow.

The skill's name/description frontmatter, project directory, and direct invocation follow [Claude Code's primary documentation](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/skills). No external video access is required at use time. No framework, route, screenshot tool, or dependency is assumed.

## 8. Readiness and outstanding work

**Directly verified:** catalogue titles/IDs, displayed dates/descriptions, selected auto-captions, limited playback frames, and documented Claude skill structure.

**Synthesized:** the visual-craft procedure, Numaya adaptations, prioritization, screenshot protocol, and evaluation instrument.

**Uncertain:** exact current app state, real routes/tokens/assets, actual visual improvement, independent user preference, current implementations of example third-party apps, and causal commercial outcomes.

**Ready to test:** inspect and map the five categories in the real repository, capture baselines, diagnose three concrete weaknesses per screen, implement isolated improvements, and produce matched screenshot comparisons. No keep recommendation exists yet.

The finished artifact is a testable design decision aid, not proof that an agent has acquired a senior designer's taste. Its value must be judged by the real five-screen pilot.

## 9. Artifact validation and limited rehearsal

The package was checked for required files, frontmatter, local reference links, date eligibility, and ZIP contents. An independent agent was given the skill and an Interests-screen task without a repository or screenshots. It correctly produced an inspection/evaluation plan, preserved the brand constraints, and marked visual outcomes unverified. This tests the workflow's evidence boundary only; it is not a redesign trial and does not demonstrate better rendered UI. The five-screen pilot remains pending.
