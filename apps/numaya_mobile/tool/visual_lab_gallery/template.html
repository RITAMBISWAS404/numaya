How to review

Each group shows the current-rules baseline O and its alternatives on the same sample data and viewport. Pick one per group, or Keep current (O), or leave it Undecided. The Proposed badge is Claude’s recommendation, not your approval, and nothing is preselected. Choosing O never restores reconnection or the old Compliment placement: the required changes apply to every option.

Images are real Flutter screenshots from the Android emulator (Pixel 10, API 36, debug build), driven by integration_test/visual_lab_gallery_test.dart; “crop” images are the family rendered alone in the debug Design Lab, “context” images are the real screen with that variant chosen. Sample insights and people are demo data. Choices are saved in this browser where possible; the exported JSON is the record to send back.
