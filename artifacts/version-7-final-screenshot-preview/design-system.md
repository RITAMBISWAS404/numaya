# Numaya V7 design system (Tiimo-inspired experiment)

This is the visual system behind `?theme=tiimo` in V7. I defined it before changing any screen, and
the screens were then brought into line with it. It lives in three places:

- **Tokens:** `tokens/numaya.tokens.json`, generated by `npm run tokens`.
- **The component layer:** `src/design-system/styles/theme-tiimo.css`.
- **The flow layer:** `src/flows/theme-tiimo.css`.

Without the flag nothing changes: the approved design renders as before.

## 1. Tokens and roles

V7 keeps the V6 palette and adds three roles for surfaces. Every themed colour is a token. No
screen uses a raw hex value.

| Role (CSS variable) | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--color-palette-canvas` → `surface-page` | **`#F6F5F1`** (new) | The canvas: the base of every themed screen and sheet |
| `--color-palette-card` → `surface` | `#FFFFFF` | Raised cards, outlined groups, floating controls, incoming bubbles |
| `--color-palette-fill-on-canvas` → `surface-soft` on the canvas | **`#EDEBE5`** (new, derived) | Recessed fills on the canvas: fields, search, the composer, unselected pills |
| `--color-palette-fill-on-card` → `surface-soft` inside white | **`#F1F0EA`** (the V6 Background, re-roled) | Recessed fills inside white cards and groups: unselected pills, the category tag, hover |
| `--color-palette-border` | `#E6E3DD` | Outlines and dividers |
| `--color-palette-brand` | `#7262C7` | Primary action, selection, the active tab icon, SuperSwipe, your messages |
| `--color-palette-purple-tint` | `#E5E3FA` | The active tab item only |
| `--color-palette-muted` | `#F2F0FE` | Supporting lavender tiles: the profile prompt and info cards |
| `--color-palette-text` | `#28262D` | Headings and body text |
| `--color-palette-secondary` | `#77737D` | Secondary text inside white surfaces |
| `--color-palette-secondary-strong` | `#6B6771` (derived in V6) | Secondary text on the canvas and recessed fills |

`surface-soft` and `text-muted` are contextual. The `.theme-tiimo` root sets the canvas values, and
white surfaces (`.card`, `.list-group__items`, `.option-card`, `.dialog__panel`, …) re-set them
for their contents. One component therefore renders correctly on either surface without variants.

### The background change: `#F1F0EA` → `#F6F5F1`

**Why:** V6's `#F1F0EA` read as beige across a full screen and made the app feel tinted rather
than light.

**How it was chosen:** I rendered four candidates side by side on Likes, Discover filters, Your
prompt and Basics ([qa/background-study.png](qa/background-study.png)).

| Candidate | Result |
|---|---|
| `#F1F0EA` (V6) | Reads as beige; white cards stand out strongly (1.14:1), but the whole screen is warm |
| `#F4F3EF` | A half step: lighter, but the canvas still reads warm against the white rows (1.11:1) |
| **`#F6F5F1`** | **Chosen.** Reads as an off-white, warm only next to pure white. White cards still separate (1.09:1) with the help of the card shadow or a hairline. Secondary text keeps AA with the derived `#6B6771` (5.06:1) |
| `#F8F7F4` | White rows and groups nearly vanish (1.07:1) without heavier shadows or borders, which would work against the restraint brief |

The canvas sits one perceptible step below white. The recessed fill `#EDEBE5` sits one step below
the canvas (1.09:1), so fields read as wells without needing borders.

## 2. Surface hierarchy: five levels

| Level | Role | Fill | Edge | Shadow | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Canvas** | `#F6F5F1` | none | none | Screen and sheet background; list rows on Messages sit directly on it |
| 2 | **Raised content card** | `#FFFFFF` | none | `--shadow-tiimo-card` (≤ 9% opacity, diffuse) | Profile sections on Discover and Like back, activity cards, profile readiness |
| 3 | **Recessed input** | `#EDEBE5` on the canvas, `#F1F0EA` inside white | none (0px border) | none | Text fields, search, the composer, the prompt answer |
| 4 | **Outlined group** | `#FFFFFF` | 1px `#E6E3DD` inset | none | Settings and filter groups (`list-group`), radio and checkbox rows (`option-card`) |
| 5 | **Floating control** | `#FFFFFF` or brand | none | `--shadow-tiimo-float` (the only strong shadow) | Tab bar, floating back and close buttons, Pass, Like, SuperSwipe, the FAB, sheets |

### When to use a shadow, a border or neither

- **Shadow:** only when the surface carries content about a person or an activity (level 2), or
  floats above scrolling content (level 5). A shadow means "this is an object".
- **Border:** groups of controls and selectable rows (level 4). They belong to the page's flow, so
  they are outlined, not lifted.
- **Neither:** the canvas, recessed inputs, pills, Muted tiles (the prompt and info cards) and
  conversation rows. These are separated by fill and spacing.

The e2e suite enforces this with an **elevation allowlist**. On every themed screen, any element
with a visible outer shadow must be one of: card, activity card, tab bar, floating nav button,
FAB, action bar button, count badge, sheet panel, primary button, match avatars or toast.
Option rows, conversation rows, the composer, filter groups and the secondary button lost the
shadows they had in V6 (see [qa/surface-audit-v6-v7.txt](qa/surface-audit-v6-v7.txt)).

## 3. Inputs

- **At rest:** the recessed fill (`#EDEBE5` on the canvas, `#F1F0EA` in white groups), no
  border, no shadow, 16px corner.
- **Focus:** the approved treatment: a white fill with the 2px brand ring. Moving from a recess
  to white plus a ring is unmistakable.
- **Label and helper:** text `#28262D` (12.53:1 on the recess); helper and placeholder
  secondary-strong `#6B6771` (4.63:1 on the recess).
- **Error and disabled:** the approved states are unchanged.

Tiimo's own inputs are outlined. Numaya's are recessed instead because the brief asked for
slightly darker inputs without shadows. This also keeps fields clearly different from the outlined
groups (level 4) they often sit next to.

## 4. Pills (emoji-and-text tags only)

| State | On the canvas | Inside a white card |
|---|---|---|
| Unselected | `#EDEBE5`, no border, no tint, no gradient; label `#28262D` | `#F1F0EA`, no border |
| Selected | **Solid brand `#7262C7`**, white semibold label, white minus or check glyph (4.90:1); no ring | same |
| Locked (at the limit) | the unselected fill with `text-subtle` label and glyph | — |

- Selected and unselected pills share their size and radius (12px; 8px on the 27px activity
  tag): only the colour changes.
- The state is never colour-only. The glyph flips from plus to minus or check, and the input
  carries `aria-pressed` or `checked`.
- Filter pills without emoji (Likes, Messages, Discover filters) keep the approved component and
  are unchanged, as in V5 and V6. See
  [comparisons/pills-only-v6-v7.png](comparisons/pills-only-v6-v7.png).

## 5. Buttons

| Variant | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Primary (`btn--primary`, FAB) | Solid brand `#7262C7`, white label (4.90:1); the float shadow because it is the main action |
| Secondary (`btn--secondary`) | White with a 1px `#E6E3DD` outline, ink label; no shadow (V6 had one) |
| Tertiary (`btn--quiet`, links) | No fill; ink label, the recessed fill on hover. Brand-coloured link text uses deep purple on the canvas (brand alone is 4.49:1) |
| Destructive (`btn--destructive`, `-solid`) | Approved semantics unchanged: danger text, danger-tint on hover, solid danger for confirmations |

Disabled, pressed and loading states are the approved ones in every case.

## 6. Purple

Purple means **act or chosen**. It appears only on:

- primary buttons and the FAB;
- SuperSwipe;
- selected pills, radios, checkboxes, toggles and sliders;
- the active tab;
- your messages;
- verified marks.

The Muted lavender tile is reserved for the prompt and info cards.

Purple is not used for:

- headings or section labels;
- unselected states;
- borders of non-selected objects;
- backgrounds of whole sections.

[qa/purple-usage.txt](qa/purple-usage.txt) counts every purple fill, outline, text and icon on each
screen. The only change from V6 is that selected pills became solid fills and lost their outline
and separate purple glyph, which removed three purple marks per selected pill.

## 7. Shapes and preserved V5 work

- **Unchanged in V7:**
  - every component radius ([qa/shape-audit.txt](qa/shape-audit.txt) is identical to V6);
  - the Discover photo fade and the solid band for missing photos;
  - the star SuperSwipe and the Pass and Like shapes;
  - the scallop-clipped Match photo with the rounded-square avatar on its lower left;
  - no heart, halo or confetti.
- **Shape tokens:**
  - `--tiimo-radius-feature` (24px): cards and groups;
  - `--tiimo-radius-item` (16px): fields, option rows and photo slots.

## 8. Contrast (measured)

| Pair | Ratio |
|---|---|
| Text `#28262D` on the canvas / recess / fill-on-card | 13.70 / 12.53 / 13.09 |
| Secondary-strong `#6B6771` on the canvas / recess / fill-on-card | 5.06 / 4.63 / 4.84 |
| Secondary `#77737D` on white | 4.63 (on the canvas it would be 4.25, so it is not used there) |
| White on brand | 4.90 |
| Brand on the canvas | 4.49, so link text on the canvas uses the deep purple instead |
| Non-text: white on the canvas / recess on the canvas / border on white | 1.09 / 1.09 / 1.28 |

The non-text pairs are reinforced by the card shadow and the outlines. None of them carries
meaning alone.

The rendered audit checked 310 text elements on 15 themed screens against the solid colour behind
each one: **0 below AA** ([qa/contrast-audit.txt](qa/contrast-audit.txt)).

## 9. States

| State | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Selected | Pills: solid brand. Rows: the 2px brand ring replaces the 1px outline. Tab: purple tint behind the brand icon |
| Focus | The approved focus-visible ring on every control; fields go from recessed to white with a brand ring |
| Pressed and hover | Recessed fill on quiet buttons and rows; the approved hover colour on primary |
| Disabled | Approved opacity and `text-subtle`; locked pills keep their fill and dim their label |

I opened only these screens through the Mobbin plugin. Full-size downloads are blocked from this
environment, so they were judged from preview images.

- [Settings](https://mobbin.com/screens/e824a18f-ebeb-489e-9b3c-aedc2a0fd9ba): rows directly on the canvas. Basis for conversation rows without cards.
- [Notifications & Sounds](https://mobbin.com/screens/87cc8a03-9705-4ab5-b954-83d29a57617f): white grouped panels with hairline dividers and no visible shadow. Basis for level 4.
- [Explore search](https://mobbin.com/screens/97468712-336b-4e0e-9de7-17645cfee1ce): a shadowless search field and a segmented control on a grey track.
- [Tasks](https://mobbin.com/screens/2e46be51-16fe-4f8e-bdc8-4d920bed12c8): task cards with a very soft shadow. Basis for the restrained level 2.
- [Me](https://mobbin.com/screens/541ed88a-c239-4c59-af24-1db6c2406b9b): an outlined list group and a floating tab bar with a shadow. Basis for levels 4 and 5.
- [Habit chips](https://mobbin.com/screens/37f58a3d-1e1e-48a0-8a6c-167af5250b49) (V5 reference): Tiimo selects with a light tint and an outline. **V7 deliberately departs from it**, as the brief asked, with a solid brand fill.

## 11. Decisions that supersede earlier rules

- **Warm recessed fill.** V2's "no warm fill" rule is superseded. The user's V6 palette direction
  is warm, and a cool grey recess looked like a different product on the warm canvas. `#EDEBE5`
  is the canvas darkened along its own hue.
- **Selected pills.** V6's tint-plus-outline selection is replaced by the solid brand fill.
