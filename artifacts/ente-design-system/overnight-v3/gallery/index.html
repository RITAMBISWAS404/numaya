Someone liked you back: recognise who it is, feel the moment, then open the chat (or view their profile, or keep browsing).

Flutter golden harness on Linux (not a device). 390x844 logical at 2x (780x1688 px), text 100%; narrow 320x693 at 2x, text 200%; tall 390x1400 at 2x for scroll continuation. Current = frozen checkpoint e60504e (tag numaya-ente-design-system-finalized-20261106-0624); A/B/C and refined control = branch experiment/numaya-ente-delight-v3-20261106-0624 at 0f5dd7c. Order per option: phone, narrow 200% text, tall continuation.

Recommendation (not selected): A: Paired portraits keeps the person as the hero while adding the mutual "you and them" meaning, with no new feature. B is a safe fallback if photo recognisability matters most; C is best kept for empty states.

Current · Frozen checkpoint

The pre-run candidate, exactly as tagged. A, B and C are built on the refined candidate, so they also carry this run's baseline corrections (V3-D01, V3-D02, V3-F01); open "Refined control" below to see the same layout with only those corrections.

A · Paired portraits

Axis: relationship: both people as two tilted portrait cards with a heart on the seam; headline names both. Says "the two of you" without words; familiar from Bumble. Costs: shows your own photo too (placeholder-heavy until real photos exist) and two cards are smaller than one.

B · Identity-led photo hero

Axis: scale and immersion: the person's photo fills 54% of the height with name, age and verification on it, as on their Discover card. Most recognisable: the match looks exactly like the card you liked. Costs: the photo dominates, the moment reads like a profile, and shared interests drop below the fold on short phones.

C · Restrained cat celebration

Axis: emotional tone through illustration: the celebrating cat leads; small round avatars and shared interests in one card. Warmest and lightest; the cat never covers a face. Costs: people are small, so the identity is weaker; the cat may feel playful for some users.
